# Financial Risk & Fraud Intelligence Platform

## Overview

The Financial Risk & Fraud Intelligence Platform is an end-to-end fintech analytics project designed to address two major challenges in today's financial ecosystem:

1. Helping investors understand and manage financial risk.
2. Detecting potentially fraudulent transactions using data-driven techniques.

With the rapid growth of retail investing and digital payments, users are generating massive amounts of financial data every day. However, most retail investors do not have access to professional-grade risk analysis tools, and fintech companies continue to face significant losses due to fraudulent activities. This project aims to bridge that gap by combining data analytics, machine learning, quantitative finance, cloud technologies, and AI-powered insights into a single platform.

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of people invest in stocks, mutual funds, and other financial products without fully understanding the risks associated with their portfolios. At the same time, fintech companies process millions of transactions daily, making fraud detection increasingly challenging.

The goal of this project is to build a platform that can:

* Analyze financial and transaction data
* Identify suspicious or high-risk activities
* Evaluate portfolio performance and risk
* Generate meaningful insights using quantitative models
* Provide explainable, AI-driven financial summaries

---

## Features

### Fraud Detection & Risk Scoring

* Detect potentially fraudulent transactions using machine learning models
* Assign risk scores to transactions
* Highlight suspicious patterns and anomalies

### Portfolio Risk Analytics

* Analyze historical market data
* Measure portfolio performance and volatility
* Track risk metrics and investment behavior

### Quantitative Finance Models

* Time Value of Money (TVM)
* CAPM (Capital Asset Pricing Model)
* Sharpe Ratio
* Monte Carlo Simulation
* Portfolio Optimization

### Financial Intelligence Dashboard

* Interactive KPI tracking
* Risk monitoring dashboards
* Business-friendly visualizations

### AI-Powered Financial Copilot

* Explain financial insights in simple language
* Summarize portfolio performance
* Generate risk and investment reports
* Assist users in understanding financial decisions
